Data & Deletion

Clear your game data

In Android Settings, open Apps → 8448 → Storage → Clear storage. The menu names may vary by phone. This removes the current run, best scores and preferences stored by the app on that device. Support cannot restore the run.

Manage Android backups

If Android backed up app data, clearing storage or uninstalling may leave the backup intact. Use your Android or backup provider’s settings to manage it.

Review ad choices

Open Settings → Ad privacy choices in 8448 when that option is available. Changing these settings or resetting an advertising ID does not erase records already held by Google. See Google’s privacy controls for its data.

Request deletion of support email

Email 1bit.monochromacy@gmail.com, preferably from the address used for the original message. We can locate and handle correspondence held in our support mailbox, subject to any applicable retention obligations.
