An endless number puzzle for Android

Connect. Merge.
Play it backwards.

In 8448, the direction you drag matters. Link matching numbers, build bigger tiles, and set up your next move on a board that keeps refilling.

See how it plays

Three ways to link

4→4

Match

Drag across adjacent tiles of the same value to merge them. You can turn corners and link diagonally.

2→8→2

Backslide

Join two equal tiles across one other number. The middle tile slides to your starting spot. Reverse the drag to land it on the other side.

8→4→4→8

Long Link

Charge the booster with five Backslides, then connect equal ends across two smaller tiles.

Pick up where you left off

Learn the moves in the animated tutorial. Your run saves on your device, so you can close the game and come back to the same board.
