Privacy Policy

Updated November 22, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy covers 8448 - Number Puzzle Game for Android and its companion website, provided by 1-Bit Monochrome. Contact 1bit.monochromacy@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Game saves and settings

The game stores your current board, scores, tutorial progress and preferences in its local app storage so you can resume play. There are no player accounts or developer-operated cloud saves. Android may back up or restore app data depending on your device and backup settings.

Ads and privacy choices

8448 uses Google AdMob for banner ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform to show and manage privacy choices where applicable. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, app interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID where available. Google uses these data for advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. Google documents that its ads SDK encrypts data in transit using TLS.

When available, open Settings → Ad privacy choices in 8448 to review your choices. Android also provides advertising ID controls on supported devices. Google manages its own retention and privacy controls under its policies.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your address, your message and any attachments. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and handle follow-up. Gmail handles delivery and storage. We keep correspondence while needed for support, follow-up or applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by contacting us.

Website hosting

This site has no site-added cookies, analytics or forms. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process request information, such as IP addresses, to operate and protect its service. External links are subject to the destination’s privacy practices.

Retention and deletion

Local progress stays on your device until removed or replaced. See Data & Deletion for device storage, backups, advertising controls and support email requests.
